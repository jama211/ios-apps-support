Privacy Policy for Light Pen

Last updated: 20th of November 2026

Information We Collect

Light Pen does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own, and I never see your notes, drawings, or anything else you make in it.

Data Storage & iCloud

Your notes and drawings are saved as files in the Light Pen folder of your own iCloud Drive, so they sync between your devices. iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and only you can access those files. If iCloud Drive is off, your notes are stored only on your device. Light Pen also keeps up to ten recent backup copies of each note in a Backups folder inside the Light Pen folder.

A few things stay only on your device: your settings, the undo history of your notes, and your progress in the Secret Text Adventure.

You can delete any note in the app. To erase everything, delete the Light Pen folder in iCloud Drive (for example, in the Files app) and delete the app.

Importing & Exporting

When you import or export a note, Light Pen only reads or writes the file you choose in the system's file picker.

In-App Purchases

Pro packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and Light Pen never receives your payment details. The app only asks the App Store which packs you own, so it can unlock them.

Third-Party Services

Light Pen does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, advertising frameworks, or remote diagnostic services.

Changes to This Policy
