Where are my notes kept?

Your notes are plain files in the Light Pen folder of your iCloud Drive, so they sync between your devices and you can open them in the Files app. If iCloud Drive is off, they're kept on your device instead. Light Pen also keeps up to ten recent backup copies of each note in a Backups folder inside the Light Pen folder.

My notes aren't syncing

Check that you're signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is on for Light Pen (Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Drive). If you're on mobile data, check that iCloud Drive is allowed to use it (Settings → Mobile Data → iCloud Drive), or wait until you're back on Wi-Fi.

Pro packs

Pro packs are optional one-time purchases: extra themes and fonts, more sketching tools, and the Secret Text Adventure, or all three in the Everything Pack. Open Settings in Light Pen and tap the Pro row at the top to see them.

To restore packs you've already bought (on a new device, or after reinstalling), open the Pro packs from Settings and tap Restore.

Where's the Secret Text Adventure?

Once you've unlocked it, open Settings in Light Pen and tap Play Secret Text Adventure. Your progress is saved as you play, and you can close it at any time.
