Privacy Policy for Daily Cash

Last updated: 5th of November 2026

Information We Collect

Daily Cash does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no servers, and no network features. Everything you enter, such as your daily amount, budget settings, and logged purchases and income, stays on your device.

Data Storage & App Groups

Your entries and settings are stored locally on your device. A small summary of your balance settings is also stored within your private iOS App Group sandbox solely so the Daily Cash Home Screen widget can display your current balance. No data leaves your device. You can erase everything at any time using Delete All Data in the app's Settings, or by deleting the app.

Third-Party Services

Daily Cash does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, advertising frameworks, or remote diagnostic services.
